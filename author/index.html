The author behind REBOUND.

About the author

Onu Sol
Adah.

The author puts ink to paper in rarely conventional but undeniably evocative ways.

01The writer

A voice shaped by humanity, faith, and lived resilience.

ONU SOL ADAH is a Nigerian writer with more than half a decade of experience writing professionally for world-renowned organizations and clients.

A former Creative Writer for one of the most impactful law firms in the United States, The Chidolue Law Firm, Atlanta, Georgia, he also worked as an Intake Coordinator and Legal Assistant with the firm during that period.

As the only Nigerian writer with the firm at the time, his in-depth, emotion-resonating writing style stood his work out and helped a number of the firm’s clients receive needed interventions from the U.S. government.

01.

Humanitarian at heart

An ardent humanitarian, lover of God, lover of music, fitness and style enthusiast, his writing transcends time and evokes hope within hearts.

02.

Stories with consequence

His firsthand experience with survivors of life’s downturns and abuse helped turn stories of tragedy into tenable legal and humanitarian documents of hope.

03.

The work now

Currently based in Nigeria, he works on biopic writings, humanitarian stories, and leads the ROAR Foundation.

The book

Why REBOUND matters.

In this current book, his firsthand experience interfacing with survivors of life’s downturns, coupled with his own personal journey of trials and triumphs, prepared him to author a book of this importance.

In REBOUND, he delivers a time-tested, battle-tested, and life-tested blueprint for crucial rebounds and comebacks across spheres of life.
