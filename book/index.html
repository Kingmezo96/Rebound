A book about rising against the odds.

REBOUND by Onu Sol Adah is a compelling book of rising against the odds — a work of resilience, undying spirit, tenacity, and unwavering determination amid opposing realities.

The book speaks to the stark reality of adversity, the ache of grief, the discovery of purpose after pain, and the spiritual rebound needed when every human pillar seems to break.

From its opening memoir to the Rebound Blueprint, the book stages a personal and universal comeback story: the fall, the fire, the courage to face reality, and the decision to rise again.

REBOUND · Opening & Memoir

Staging the greatest comeback story of life.

Read Memoir ↗

For you.

To everyone who has been knocked down, broken by a season in life, counted out, or written off — this is your book.

Your story is not over. Your rebound begins here.

When life deals some of the harshest combinations of odds, and it feels as though you are locked in a drawback — when certain realities pose setbacks with almost no glimpse of hope for a comeback — you must realize that the further backward an arrow is pulled, the further forward and upward its velocity when released.

This book is that proverbial release: from a drawback into a comeback.

Introduction

The fall isn’t the finish line.

Every great story has a low point. The hero gets crushed, loses everything, doubts themselves, and then — against all odds — rises. This book is that rising.

Whether you lost a job opportunity, a relationship, a business, a career, a partner, your faith, a loved one, a marriage, your health, an investment, your reputation, or your sense of worth, the last of you has not been heard. Your story is still being written.

Your response to life’s situations is what determines whether they break you or make you. You do not run away from it. You face it, brace it, and defy it. You do not respond with fear; you respond with fierce faith.

Chapter One

Stark Reality.

In the cold, windy hours of an unforgettable night in August — shaky hands and trembling feet — he stood outside the building where he had just received the shattering news that he had been cut off from work.

The cold winds whistled over his face. Too many thoughts surged through his mind like hail, but like a duck on a pond — motionless on the surface, yet paddling beneath the murky water — he worked quickly for solutions.

The stark reality of the moment grew stronger by the second. Standing there in the middle of the night, needing quick answers in real time, it felt like a bolstering storm with no harbor in sight.

Rebound cue

Out of the fall came an unstoppable rise.

Hidden in the elements of the fall lie the elements of the rise. The fuel needed to propel your rebound upward is still found in the very force that seems to pull you downward.

There is no rebound without an audacious resolve to defy fear, own the moment, and defy the norm.
