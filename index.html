About
the author.

The author puts ink to paper in rarely conventional but undeniably evocative ways.

ONU SOL ADAH is a Nigerian writer and humanitarian storyteller whose work turns difficult realities into language of courage, dignity, and hope.

Through REBOUND, he brings a time-tested and life-tested voice to the subject of resilience, purpose, faith, and rising again.
