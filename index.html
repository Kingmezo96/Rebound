04Behind the work

About
the author.

The author puts ink to paper in rarely conventional but undeniably evocative ways.

ONU SOL ADAH is a Nigerian writer with more than half a decade of experience writing professionally for world-renowned organizations and clients.

A former Creative Writer for one of the most impactful law firms in the United States, The Chidolue Law Firm, Atlanta, Georgia, he also worked as an Intake Coordinator and Legal Assistant with the firm during that period. As the only Nigerian writer with the firm at the time, his in-depth, emotion-resonating writing style stood his work out and helped a number of the firm’s clients receive needed interventions from the U.S. government.

An ardent humanitarian at heart, a lover of God, a lover of music, and a fitness and style enthusiast, his writing transcends time and evokes hope within hearts.

Currently based in Nigeria, he works on biopic writings, humanitarian stories, and leads the ROAR Foundation. In this current book, his firsthand experience interfacing with survivors of life’s downturns and survivors of abuse — writing their stories and turning what could have remained tragedy into tenable legal and humanitarian documents of hope for needed interventions — coupled with his own personal journey of trials and triumphs, prepared him to author a book of this importance.

In REBOUND, he delivers a time-tested, battle-tested, and life-tested blueprint for crucial rebounds and comebacks across spheres of life.
