THE FALL

Rebound is a compelling book of rising against the odds: resilience, undying spirit, tenacity, and unwavering determination amid opposing realities.

It follows the audacity to defy adversity, not as a slogan, but as a lived rhythm: falling, facing the pressure, and choosing to rise with clearer purpose.
