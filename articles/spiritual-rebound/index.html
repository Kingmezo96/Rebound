Spiritual rebound.

A complete rebound cannot exist only on the material or physical plane. REBOUND argues that a person can have money, strength, and public success, yet remain hollow if the spirit is fractured and detached from a higher source.

Severe trauma often strips away surface confidence. When human pillars break, a person is forced to look upward and ask deeper questions about meaning, surrender, and divine order.

Faith inside the burning room

Faith in REBOUND is not denial. It is the radical choice to believe there is purpose working through life even when a person is standing in the middle of a burning room.

This makes spiritual rebound one of the book’s deepest themes: prayerfulness, surrender, and reconnecting with the source that gives strength beyond human effort.
