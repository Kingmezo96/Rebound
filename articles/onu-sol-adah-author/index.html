Onu Sol Adah, author of REBOUND.

Onu Sol Adah is a Nigerian writer, professional storyteller, speech writer, philanthropist, and author of REBOUND. His work is marked by emotional precision and a deep interest in stories of adversity, survival, faith, and purpose.

He has written speeches for a World Bank executive and top Nigerian government officials. He also served as a former Intake Coordinator and Legal Assistant at The Chidolue Law Firm in Atlanta, Georgia.

The writer behind the book

His experience with legal and humanitarian storytelling helped shape the voice of REBOUND. The book carries the texture of lived pressure: grief, responsibility, spiritual searching, and the courage to rise again.
