Rising against the odds.

Rising against the odds is not a clean upward line. It is often slow, private, and fiercely practical. REBOUND by Onu Sol Adah gives language to that climb.

The book speaks of resilience, undying spirit, tenacity, and unwavering determination amid opposing realities. It is written for people who have been pressed by circumstances and still need to find movement.

The discipline of the comeback

A rebound is not merely a return. It is a transformation. You do not return exactly as you were. You return different, clearer, and often more useful to others.

That makes REBOUND a strong fit for readers searching for a rising against the odds book, a resilience book, or a practical reflection on comeback stories.
