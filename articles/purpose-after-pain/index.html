Purpose after pain.

REBOUND uses the image of Kintsugi, the Japanese art of repairing broken pottery with gold, to describe purpose after pain. The broken place is not hidden. It is illuminated.

This is one of the most important ideas in the book: the brokenness can birth purpose. The scars no longer have to remain marks of shame. They can become credentials, proof that a person walked through fire and refused to burn to ash.

When scars become credentials

Purpose after pain does not erase the past. It gives the past a new assignment. It turns survival into service, memory into meaning, and pain into a deeper capacity to help others.

Readers looking for a book about purpose, resilience, and comeback stories will find REBOUND to be a clear and emotionally grounded guide.
