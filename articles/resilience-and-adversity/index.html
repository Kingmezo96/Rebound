A book about resilience and adversity.

REBOUND by Onu Sol Adah is written for the reader who has met adversity closely and still feels called to rise. It is not a shallow motivational slogan; it is a book about resilience, pressure, grief, courage, purpose, and the disciplined work of becoming whole again.

The word “rebound” suggests movement after impact. In the book, that movement is emotional, spiritual, practical, and deeply human. A person may lose a job, face grief, confront uncertainty, or feel abandoned by every visible support system. REBOUND speaks into that moment with language that is direct, cinematic, and grounded in lived experience.

Why resilience matters

Resilience is not pretending that pain did not happen. It is the decision to keep building after the breaking. REBOUND frames resilience as the strength to carry memory, pressure, responsibility, and hope at the same time.

For readers searching for a book about resilience and adversity, REBOUND offers a human blueprint: face the stark reality, process the grief, recover purpose, reconnect with faith, and rise again with a different kind of strength.
